How the comparisons work
  • CET is the Central England daily mean temperature. EWP is England and Wales precipitation.
  • Figures use observations only, through each series’ displayed cut-off date. Forecasts are excluded.
  • Each historical year is compared over matching calendar dates. Only years with complete coverage enter rankings; a tie shares the same rank.
  • The reference average is 1991–2020. If coverage is incomplete, the available reference-year count is shown.
  • Sunshine is a monthly total in hours for the selected region. Year-to-date totals compare January through the latest confirmed month in each year. Published complete annual totals are retained; incomplete windows are unranked. The sunshine dropdown applies to its summary, monthly chart and figures.
  • For temperature and rainfall, 29 February is omitted from period averages, totals and rankings so all years use the same days. Daily highlights retain all observed dates.
  • The dry run counts consecutive days with EWP below 1 mm. Missing observations break a run.
