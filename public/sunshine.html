About these comparisons
  • Values are total sunshine hours. Only complete comparison periods receive a rank; ties share the same rank. Incomplete periods use hollow diamonds.
  • Year/period-to-date compares the same complete months in every year, through the latest confirmed month. Daily sunshine data is not available here.
  • Winter includes the preceding December and is labelled by its January/February year. Hex seasons are consecutive two-month periods starting in January.
  • Published annual and seasonal totals are retained. Hex seasons, partial periods and periods completed by terminal updates are sums of the monthly totals; rounding can cause small differences from published aggregates.
  • The server combines the archived Met Office files with confirmed monthly updates. Refresh to load new confirmations.
